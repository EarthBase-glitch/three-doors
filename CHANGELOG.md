# Changelog

Daily additions to Three Doors, one per realm per day. See NOTES-FOR-OWNER.md for any manual Firebase console steps a given day's update requires.

## 2026-09-09

- **Heaven — "The Register."** A second narrative ladder inside the existing Memories screen, alongside the streak-based visions from 2026-09-02. Where those are earned by *consecutive* days, The Register is earned by total lifetime completions of Heaven's daily task, gaps and all — reaching 5, 15, then 40 total returns (streak resets don't erase progress) unlocks a one-time piece of narrative about a light that keeps a record of every return, not a scoreboard of unbroken days. Directly literalizes Heaven's tagline ("The light does not judge") as a mechanic: the thing it's not judging is exactly the streak-breaking the other ladder is strict about.
- **Reincarnation — "Past Lives."** A second ladder gated on a mechanic that previously had zero narrative attached to it: spending points (or using a free "escape") to move to a different door. Every switch that lands you back in Reincarnation now counts toward a lifetime "rebirths" total; the 1st, 3rd, and 6th such switch unlocks a one-time vision about the body and habits carried over from a life you don't consciously remember. This gives the existing move-door utility an actual story payoff for the first time.
- **Hell — "The Reckoning."** A third ladder, gated on how many times an account's streak has genuinely broken (had a real streak going, then missed a day) while Hell is the current door — 1st, 3rd, and 7th fall each unlock a one-time vision about a fire that was never counting the streak itself, only how many times you came back after slipping. Fits "The fire remembers your name" more literally than the streak ever did, since the streak forgets on every reset and this doesn't.

All three reuse the exact same locked/unlocked list UI, storage shape (`unlockedChronicles` array), and "new content" nav dot that the 2026-09-02 Memories/visions system already established — no new screen, no new nav button, no new Firestore collection. New fields (`totalCompletions`, `resets`, `doorSwitches`, `unlockedChronicles`) live on the existing `dailytasks/{accountId}` doc, which was already fully open to read/write, so no `firestore.rules` change was needed.

## 2026-09-03

- **All three realms — Explore (a walkable area per realm).** Added a new "Explore" screen, reachable from the character page next to Today's tasks. It's a small top-down area — genuinely new tech for this project: written in C++ and compiled to WebAssembly (`game/explore.cpp` → `game/explore.js` / `game/explore.wasm` via Emscripten), rendered on a `<canvas>` with SDL2. Each realm gets its own hand-placed map, wall layout, and color palette (Heaven: a ring of pale pillars; Reincarnation: tilled rows of soil; Hell: a cramped, narrow maze) — move with WASD or arrow keys, walk into walls and you're stopped (real collision, not decorative), and walking onto one of the small glowing markers surfaces a short line of realm-flavored text, in the same terse second-person voice as the rest of the site. Also playable on phones: a small on-screen D-pad (`explore_set_touch` on the C++ side) appears in the corner of the canvas for touch devices only — CSS-gated to coarse/no-hover pointers, so keyboard users never see it — and drives the same movement/collision code as WASD. The world also isn't static anymore: ambient particles drift per realm (rising embers in Hell, floating motes in Heaven, pollen in Reincarnation), markers pulse instead of sitting fixed, and the player has an idle/walk bob plus a facing dot. No persistence yet — it's pure atmosphere, not tied to streaks or points. See `game/README.md` for how to rebuild it.
- **All three realms — "Enter game" 3D scenes.** Each of the 9 markers across the three realms (3 per realm) now offers an "Enter game" option alongside its caption. Picking it opens a genuine first-person 3D scene — one per marker, hand-built to match that marker's specific line of flavor text (e.g. Heaven's "the light here doesn't cast a shadow" is an overexposed white room; Hell's "quieter in the corners, that's aim" has four dim embered braziers in the corners and a darker center; Reincarnation's mismatched row is a crop grid with one row a different color). Built with three.js (loaded from cdnjs, r128), independent of the 2D wasm engine — drag to look around, WASD or the same on-screen D-pad to walk, with room-boundary collision. "Back to Explore" returns to the 2D area. See the `SCENE3D` config and `Explore3DScreen` in `index.html` for all nine scene definitions.
- **Heaven's "Something up here is still being built" — rebuilt around real research and real images.** Pulled a survey of afterlife/paradise myth across traditions (Elysium, Jannah, Svarga, Aaru, the Pure Land, and more) — the recurring throughline was light, a garden-or-city "paradise perfected," and standing above the clouds. The scene now stages that literally: John Martin's 1851 painting *The Plains of Heaven* (public domain) as a distant backdrop, glimpsed through an unfinished wireframe gate, standing on a real cloud photograph (CC0, via Wikimedia/Unsplash) instead of a flat floor color — the finished vision, framed by the thing still being built in front of it. Added real texture-mapping support to the 3D engine for this (`backdrop` / `floorTexture` in `SCENE3D`, `TextureLoader` in `Explore3DScreen`, with proper texture disposal on unmount). Both images are verified public-domain/CC0 (not just "free" from a random search) — sources and license details in `game/textures/heaven/CREDITS.md`.
- **All nine 3D scenes — "the room isn't the same twice."** The static-diorama feeling of the 3D scenes got a real retention mechanic behind it instead of just more decoration, based on a pass through game-retention psychology (Hook Model, variable reward, curiosity gaps): every scene now shifts with the visitor's local clock — four lighting/mood phases a day (dawn/day/dusk/night: color tint, dimmer and cooler at night, brighter particles) applied uniformly via `TIME_PHASES`/`tintColor()` — and one small sacred-geometry symbol (Flower of Life, Tree of Life, Merkaba, or Metatron's Cube — built procedurally from primitives, no external assets, tinted to each room's own palette) sits at a per-scene spot and rotates daily on the same `dayOfYear` schedule the daily tasks use. Nothing here is driven by player action — the whole point is it's outside their control, so the only way to know what a scene looks like right now is to go look. A new tagline in the 3D screen ("This place doesn't hold still...") makes sure that's actually legible rather than a mechanic nobody notices.

## 2026-09-02

- **All three realms — Memories (milestone visions).** Added a "Memories" screen, reachable from the character page next to Lore and Today's tasks. It's a per-realm ladder of one-time narrative unlocks tied to the existing streak counter: reaching a 3-, 7-, or 30-day streak while a realm is your current door permanently unlocks that realm's vision for that milestone — a short piece of atmospheric second-person narrative, written in the game's existing voice, that you keep and can revisit forever after. Each realm has its own three-part arc (Heaven: a light that starts letting old names and a hidden seam through; Reincarnation: a body and a soil that both turn out to remember more than "starting over" implied; Hell: a fire that's learning to aim, and a voice that finally gets to be heard) — the first narrative throughline the game has had, on top of what was previously pure daily-habit/customization infrastructure. A small dot on the "Memories" nav button flags a freshly unlocked vision until it's opened. Stored as an `unlockedVisions` array on the existing `dailytasks/{accountId}` document — no new Firestore collection, and no `firestore.rules` change needed (that document was already fully open to read/write).
