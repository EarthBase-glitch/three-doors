# Door art

`hell.svg`, `earth.svg`, `heaven.svg` — procedurally generated, not
photos or AI art. `gen_doors.py` builds all three: branching
heat-fractured cracks for Hell, an L-system tree + stone arch + vines
for Reincarnation, a sunburst + symmetric ironwork scrollwork for
Heaven. Re-run it (`python3 gen_doors.py` from this directory) and
commit the regenerated SVGs if you want to tweak the look.

Used as the door-leaf background on the Hall (door-choice) screen in
`index.html`, layered under the existing swing-open animation, glow,
and label.
