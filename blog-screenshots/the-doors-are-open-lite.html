The Doors Are Open

September 2, 2026

A progress update on the small web game where you pick a door — Heaven, Hell, or Reincarnation — and build a life behind it. Screenshots included, straight from the live site.

A little while back we posted about building Three Doors from nothing — a static site, no backend framework, just a single HTML file talking to Firebase. Since then it hasn't stopped moving. Here's where things stand now, in screenshots.

The walk in

Nothing's changed about the first few seconds — you land on the threshold, step through, and give yourself a name.

Then the doors. Each one shows how many other visitors are already behind it, live.

What's grown since

The character screen used to be the whole game. Now it's the hub — your avatar, your daily task and streak, the realm's community lore, and (new) a way to support the project, all in reach without scrolling.

Every realm rotates its own daily task. Show up, complete it, keep the streak alive — miss a day and it resets.

And every realm has its own growing body of lore — short pieces submitted by visitors, reviewed before they go public, building out the mythology of each door one entry at a time.

The newest piece: keeping it running

Hosting and daily development aren't free, so Three Doors now has a quiet way to support it — entirely optional, never required to play. A one-time tip ($3, $5, or $10) or a $3/month subscription, either one manageable (and cancellable) right from the character screen. Every supporter shows up on a public leaderboard inside Soul Stats.

One-time tips are non-refundable; subscriptions can be cancelled anytime, with the current billing period standing. Full details are in the site's Terms of Service.

It works on your phone too

The whole site is responsive — same character, same streak, same doors, laid out for a smaller screen.

Happening today

Three Doors gets a small, meaningful addition to its story every single day — an automated process reads the whole codebase each morning and adds one new piece of narrative content to each realm, reviewed before it goes live. Today's addition lands at 9:00am ET — behind the doors within the hour of this post going up. Nothing to do but go check what's new once it's there.
