## plains-of-heaven.jpg

- **Work:** *The Plains of Heaven* (c. 1851), John Martin
- **Source:** [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:John_Martin_-_The_Plains_of_Heaven_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg), via Google Art Project / Tate Britain
- **License:** Public domain (painting published before 1931; PD-Art in the US). Tate Britain asserts a UK copyright claim on the digital reproduction specifically, which Wikimedia does not recognize as valid for PD-Art reproductions and neither do we for this use.
- **Used as:** the distant backdrop glimpsed through the unfinished scaffolding — the "vision" of what's being built, versus the incomplete structure in the foreground.
