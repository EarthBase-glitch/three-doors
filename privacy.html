Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2, 2026

What we collect

Three Doors is built on Firebase (Google Cloud) for storage, so all data below is stored there. Here's exactly what's collected and why:

DataWhat it isWho can see it
Sign-in name & passphraseA name you choose, and a hashed (not plaintext) version of your passphrase — this isn't a verified identity, see the Terms of ServiceYour display name is public; the passphrase hash is not shown anywhere
Door choiceWhich realm (Heaven, Hell, Reincarnation) you pickedPublic, shown as an aggregate count
CharacterSkin tone, outfit, and gender selectionVisible to you; realm population is public, individual characters are not broadcast elsewhere
Daily task progressYour streak and pointsVisible to you; not shown publicly by name
Lore submissionsText you submit, plus your display namePrivate until reviewed; public once approved
Suggestion form message & emailFeedback text and the email address you provide (required to submit)Private — only the site owner can read it
Page viewsAn aggregate daily count of site visits — no per-visitor tracking in this dataPrivate — only the site owner can read it

Third-party services

  • Firebase / Google Cloud — stores everything above.
  • Google Analytics — collects standard web analytics (pages visited, approximate location from IP, device/browser type) across the site.
  • Payment processor (if donations are enabled) — such as Stripe, which handles your payment details directly. We never see or store your card number.

Cookies & local storage

The site stores a few small preferences directly in your browser (not sent to us): your last-used sign-in name (to save you retyping it), your sound on/off preference, and your local character draft before it saves. Google Analytics also sets its own cookies, per Google's policies.

How long we keep data

Account, character, and progress data is kept as long as your account exists. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see Contact below) — because sign-in isn't verified, we'll do our best to confirm the request is really about your account before deleting it.

Children's privacy

Three Doors isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under that age. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we'll remove it.

Your choices

You can stop using the site at any time. To request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it, use the contact info below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or to request your data be deleted: mkirtondaike@gmail.com, or use the suggestion form on the character page.
